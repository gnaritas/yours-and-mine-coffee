Privacy

A simple site that watches nobody.

This site sets no cookies, loads no advertising or analytics scripts, and follows you nowhere. Here is everything it does with information:

The contact form

What you type — your name, and any phone number or email you choose to add, plus your message — is sent to the shop’s own inbox so the people behind the counter can answer. It isn’t sold, shared, or added to mailing lists by this site.

The map embed

The directions map on the contact and visit pages loads map tiles from Google, which may see your IP address when the map paints. Outside that, no third party loads anything on these pages.

Server logs

The web server keeps basic request logs (page, time, browser type) to keep the site healthy. Nothing else is recorded about visitors.

Questions

Email yoursandminecoffee@gmail.com and a person will read it.
