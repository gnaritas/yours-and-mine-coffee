How it started

It was supposed to be a cart.

Mireya Miller grew up in Kansas City with a coffee-loving family whose roots run to Zacatecas, Mexico — and she always knew she wanted a shop of her own. She and her husband, David Miller III, planned to start Yours and Mine small, as a coffee cart. Then this room on Pennsylvania Avenue came open in August, and they took the leap.

The shop opened the weekend of November 7, 2026, and the neighborhood found it fast — opening weekend sold out a week’s worth of pastries in a couple of days.

“We want it to be your third space. Your home away from home. A place to work, to linger, to meet a friend, to feel held.”— from the shop’s own Instagram

Inside the space

Fidel’s bones, new purpose

The room keeps its history. It was Fidel’s Cigar Shop before it was a coffee shop, and the wooden built-ins came with it — the main room fills with table seating, and a curtained doorway opens past the order counter to a smaller lounge.

The storefront is an inviting green; the inside is cozy dark red. Early regulars found it the moment the doors opened: the shop holds a 5.0 rating on Google.

The counter

Espresso on Oddly Correct. Syrups from our own kitchen.

Everything runs through one idea: the flavors Mireya’s family made, served the way a family serves them. Every drink — hot or iced — comes in a 12-ounce cup, so the coffee tastes like coffee.

Quick answers

Before you come by

Where exactly are you?

4112 Pennsylvania Avenue in Westport — the corner room on the avenue, painted inviting green out front, Kansas City, MO 64111.

When are you open?

Monday through Thursday 5:30 a.m.–5:00 p.m., Friday and Saturday 8:00 a.m.–8:00 p.m., Sunday 8:00 a.m.–3:00 p.m. Open every day.

What should I try first?

The fuego dulce latte — habanero and dulce de leche, $7. It leads sweet and finishes with a soft kick. If heat isn’t your thing, the lechera or the horchata are the classic pulls.

Do you have anything besides coffee?

Yes — teas, matcha, horchata, agua de jamaica and more, hot or iced, all in 12-ounce cups.

What time do you open on weekdays?

Early: 5:30 a.m., Monday through Thursday. Fridays and Saturdays start at 8:00 a.m.
