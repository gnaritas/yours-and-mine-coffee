Westport · Kansas City

A room built for lingering.

Green out front. Cozy dark red inside. Wooden built-ins that came with the room — because this room has been somebody’s corner of Westport for a long time.

The room

Fidel’s Cigar Shop, second act.

The storefront on Pennsylvania Avenue is an inviting green; step in and the room turns cozy dark red. The main room holds plenty of table seating and the wooden built-ins that came with the previous tenant — Fidel’s Cigar Shop — still standing, still holding the shelves.

Past the main room, through a doorway framed with pulled curtains, there’s a smaller lounge and the order counter. Two rooms, two moods: one to meet a friend, one to disappear into.

“A place to work, to linger, to meet a friend, to feel held.”— the shop, on its own Instagram

Mornings & nights

Weekdays start early — 5:30 a.m. Monday through Thursday — and the room runs late on Friday and Saturday, until 8 p.m. Sundays wind down by 3.

The playlist doesn’t take sides: on the shop’s first reported morning, Bad Bunny’s “KLOuFRENS” played lightly over the speakers at dawn, The Star noted.

And the regulars know: ask David for a joke.
