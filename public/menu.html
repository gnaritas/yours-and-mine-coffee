The menu

A small menu, made on purpose.

Not a wall of options — the flavors Mireya grew up with, pulled properly, sweetened the way home sweetens things. New items rotate in; the house ideas stay.

Espresso & coffee

Espresso, the way it should taste.

Every drink comes in a 12-ounce cup, hot or iced. It’s a rule with a reason: “They have to taste the coffee… I really want to keep it to 12 ounces,” Mireya says.

  • Espresso drinks on Oddly Correct beanspulled with care, every order
  • Hot or icedevery drink, 12 oz
  • House-made syrupsin-house from day one, going fully house-made
  • Rotating seasonal optionswinter lineup debuts Dec. 1

The one people drive for

Fuego dulce latte — habanero & dulce de leche.

The Kansas City Star ordered it on its first visit and reported the verdict for the hesitant: don’t fear the heat — it’s a balance of sweet up front and a complementary kick behind it.

$7 · the house signature

Latina-inspired flavors

The flavors of home, in a cup.

Lechera

Inspired by the sweet, milky cafés of Mexican home kitchens — dulce-sweet and smooth.

Dulce de leche

Caramelized milk, slow and golden — the base of the fuego dulce and a sweeten-everything move.

Horchata

The familiar cinnamon-rice refreshment, the way it’s poured at home.

Agua de jamaica

Hibiscus, cold and ruby-bright — the flower on our door, drunk.

Baked goods

The pastry case, sourced from neighbors.

The case is stocked from Heirloom in Brookside and Aurora Cafe & Bakery — local bakeries the shop turned to on opening weekend, when a week’s worth of pastries sold out in a couple of days, and kept ever since.

Come early if there’s something you’re eyeing. The case empties.
